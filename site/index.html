Lighting control where you barely program. Put your fixtures on a floor plan, pick “wave left to right” or “pulse from the centre”, and play. Art-Net, sACN and USB-DMX; Windows, macOS and Linux.

Lumap updates itself: Setup → Updates shows your version, lets you join the beta, and installs a new version when you say so — never during a show by itself.

What it does

  • Effects come from where each fixture is: waves, chases and pulses across the real room.
  • Play page built like a console: buttons and faders on pages, soft colours and positions, priorities.
  • Set up a rig by clicking the lamp that lights; import from MagicQ, WolfMix, MVR or a patch CSV.
  • 2D and 3D visualizer that can pop out to another screen.
